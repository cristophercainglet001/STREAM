What's New

Term-Based Schedule Management
Introducing Term Management for a more flexible and organized scheduling experience.

    Users can now select and manage schedules by Term 1 to Term 4, allowing them to easily filter and generate Teachers Program, Class Program, and Master Class Program reports according to the selected term.
The new feature also provides the ability to copy schedules from one term to another, making it easier to prepare succeeding terms without rebuilding schedules from scratch. This helps reduce repetitive encoding, saves valuable time, and provides a more efficient way to manage changing schedules throughout the school year.
    With Term Management, STREAM gives schools greater flexibility, better organization, and smarter schedule management—allowing teachers and school personnel to focus more on what matters most: delivering quality education.
